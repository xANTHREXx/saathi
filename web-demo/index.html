What Saathi did

Waiting for a trip…

After the trip: sealed record

1 · Trip reader

Gemini reads the Uber, Ola or Rapido notification and the plate photo. Without AI, an on-phone pattern reads the plate, or she types it.

2 · Watcher

rule on the phone, every 10 s: a stop over 5 min (10 for bus or train), 300 m off route, or late. No AI decides when to check on her.

3 · Check-in and alert

alert a loud check-in over the lock screen. No answer in 10 s: SMS from her SIM to the right contacts, a recording, and a live link.

4 · Speak for me

rule one tap says it aloud in the local language, Hindi and English, for when she freezes and can't speak.

5 · Companion

Gemini talks like an elder sister and names open places nearby. Never says she is safe, never asks what happened.

6 · Reporting guide

Gemini + Search lists her options and drafts a complaint from the sealed record. She decides and files it herself.
